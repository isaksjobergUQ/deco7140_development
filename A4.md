Task Overview

In this final work phase you will act as an individual contributor within the Connect Communities Initiative (CCI) design team. You are required to implement the design proposal you created for A3 and produce a fully functional website that reflects CCI’s mission of fostering social cohesion and supporting diverse communities. Implementation is where design concepts become a live product – you must manage trade‑offs, refine your design and meet both client expectations and technical standards.

Responsibilities

Develop a fully functional website based on your A3 design proposal. Follow your site map and style guide but document and justify any variations.

Ensure accessibility and ethics by incorporating WCAG 2.2 guidelines and addressing the ethical considerations identified earlier.

Communicate your decisions via an Implementation Rationale that documents and justifies your choices, linking them to client and user needs and UX/UI principles.

Completing this task will give you practical experience in web development, accessibility and ethical design while refining your professional skills.

Preparation

Finish work phases 1–3: you will build on this work.

Review key resources: lecture content up to Week 13; the Australian Computer Society (ACS) Code of Professional Ethics and Code of Professional Conduct; WCAG 2.2 and the “How to Meet WCAG” quick reference.

Attend Weeks 10–13 lectures/activities: they contain essential information for this phase.

Apply feedback from A3: use the feedback from your A3 design proposal to guide and improve your implementation.

What You Must Demonstrate
Implementation of a Functional Website

Build a website that reflects your A3 design proposal (including any justified variations).

Apply responsive design principles with a mobile‑first focus.

Implement at least one interactive or dynamic feature, such as a resource directory, interactive calendar or API integration.

Integration of Accessibility and Ethical Considerations

Follow WCAG 2.2 guidelines to ensure the site is usable by diverse users.

Embed ethical considerations identified during previous phases, ensuring inclusivity, cultural sensitivity and alignment with CCI’s mission.

High‑Quality Code and Development Practices

Write well‑structured, maintainable and modular HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

Follow best practices: semantic markup, clear file organisation and regular GitHub commits (at least two per week).

Deploy your site to your UQCloud zone and ensure it works online.

Use and Reflection of Generative AI Tools

Use GenAI tools thoughtfully to support creativity and productivity.

Acknowledge the use of these tools following the GenAI/MT Usage Framework.

Reflect on how GenAI tools affected your workflow and identify strategies for their effective use in future practice.

Clear and Professional Communication

Produce an Implementation Rationale explaining your development decisions, including any justified variations from A3.

Use annotations, visuals and written explanations to link decisions to client needs, user research and UX/UI principles.

Communicate clearly and professionally.

Link to A1.4 Individual Review

Your submission must also meet the coding practices assessed in A1.4. Demonstrate proper use of HTML, CSS and JavaScript, accessible code and responsive design.

Assessment Deliverables
Develop the Website

Follow your A3 site map and style guide.

Document and justify any variations you make; they must align with the client brief, meet user and accessibility needs and be explained in your Implementation Rationale.

Include the following in your site:

Responsive design with a mobile‑first approach.

Dynamic functionality (e.g. an interactive calendar or API integration).

Accessibility features meeting WCAG 2.2 Level AA.

Evidence of ethical design, especially regarding the subscription plug‑in request and other considerations from previous assessments.

Perform an accessibility audit using the IBM Equal Access Accessibility Checker, take a screenshot of the results and include it in your Implementation Rationale.

Deploy the website to your UQCloud zone in a folder named work_phase_4 with the home page named index.html.

Create the Implementation Rationale

This is a separate webpage (save as implementation_rationale.html in your work_phase_4 folder) that:

Explains alignment with client and user needs: show how the site meets the client’s goals and addresses the needs identified in your A3 proposal and how it aligns with CCI’s mission.

Discusses accessibility and ethical considerations: link back to your A2 research and explain how you integrated these principles.

Identifies and justifies variations from your A3 design proposal, explaining why changes were made (e.g. due to feasibility or technical constraints).

Reflects on challenges and strategies used during implementation.

Includes your accessibility audit screenshot and reflects on the results.

Links to your GitHub repository.

Acknowledge Your Use of GenAI

Create another webpage (genai_mt_acknowledgement.html in work_phase_4) that:

Acknowledges GenAI/MT use according to the GenAI and MT Usage Framework.

Includes the completed GenAI/MT checklist showing all framework steps followed.

Reflects on GenAI/MT usage: discuss how AI tools supported your creativity, technical skills and problem‑solving; identify challenges and strategies used; note examples where AI tools were especially effective; and outline insights for future professional use, including ethical considerations and transparency.

Includes an integrity statement affirming adherence to the framework and academic integrity.

Policies
Extensions and Late Submission

Extensions can be requested through my.UQ and are limited to a maximum of 7 days. Late submissions will incur a penalty of one grade per 24‑hour period; after 7 days the penalty is 100%.

GenAI Usage and Integrity

Using GenAI/MT tools is a key learning objective. You must demonstrate responsible and reflective use of these tools in your interview and documentation. Acknowledge how and why AI tools were used, explain how they supported your learning, critically evaluate and refine AI‑generated content, maintain your own voice and reflect on how AI influenced your learning. Failing to acknowledge AI or using it beyond allowed boundaries will trigger an academic integrity review.

Grading Criteria

The assessment will be graded against seven criteria:

Criterion Description
Website Functionality (LO2, LO3, LO4, LO5) Completeness, interactivity, responsiveness and adherence to planned features. Dynamic features should enhance the user experience while meeting accessibility and ethical standards.
Code Quality and Standards (LO3, LO5, LO6) Structure, readability and adherence to industry standards in HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Regular commits and a well‑maintained repository are required.
Integration of Accessibility Considerations (LO4, LO5) Implementation of WCAG 2.2 standards and ethical principles reflecting client and user needs.
Design Coherence (LO2, LO4, LO5) Alignment of design with CCI’s mission, client needs and user research. A cohesive UX/UI that demonstrates inclusivity and ethical representation.
Implementation Rationale (LO2, LO4, LO8) Depth of critical thinking and clarity in explaining development decisions, alignment with client/user needs and ethical considerations, and justification for variations.
GenAI/MT Usage and Reflection (LO5, LO7, LO8) Thoughtful and responsible use of AI tools, reflection on their impact, and insights for future use while maintaining academic integrity.
Professional Communication (LO2, LO4, LO8) Clarity, organisation and professionalism in the website and all documentation. Ability to convey technical concepts effectively to the intended audience.
